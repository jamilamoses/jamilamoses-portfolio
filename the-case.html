01 · The Thesis

The Thesis🎯

For the last several years I have worked at the lead level: Lead Sales Engineer at Collibra, Senior Enterprise SE at Clari, and now Senior SE at Salesforce. In each role I carried revenue, built the enablement and programs my teams depend on, and developed the engineers around me. Promotion to Lead SE would not be a move into new territory. It would recognize the scope I already own. The time is now.

01

The buyers I sell to

Multi-cloud deals sold across the full buying group, from the technical evaluator to the executive who owns the budget.

02

Technical depth, business language

Credible with engineers on the technical detail, and fluent in the revenue and outcomes leaders care about.

03

Trusted advisor

Accounts that expand and renew early, built on trust earned over time.

04 · The Plan

30 / 60 / 90 Day Plan🧭

30 Days
Establish
  • Build rapport right away, meeting 1:1 with the SEs, RMs, AEs, and practice leaders to learn the current motion and where I can add value fastest
  • Immerse in the practice, how we sell, who we sell to (operating partners, portfolio-company leaders), and where SE involvement moves an investment thesis toward operating results
  • Audit the practice's discovery frameworks and demo assets against today's Agentforce and AI-led motions, and flag what is outdated
  • Get sharp on the AI landscape for this space, what our tools do, and how we differ from the competitors these firms also evaluate
60 Days
Contribute
  • Build at least one reusable asset the whole practice can use, a discovery framework or narrative that scales across the PE firms and portfolio companies we work with
  • Lead a peer enablement session on a product area or value-creation angle I have gotten sharp on
  • Carry 2-3 strategic, multi-cloud deals as lead SE, the kind that set a repeatable pattern for the practice
  • Establish a consistent rhythm with my RMs and AEs and the broader specialist team
90 Days
Scale
  • Have a framework or asset in steady use across the practice, not only me, with a simple measure of adoption
  • Bring leadership a clear point of view on how we differentiate on business value versus our competitors
  • Become a named resource for the practice in a product area or portfolio motion
  • Formalize how I develop the SEs around me through pairing, shadowing, and feedback, and lift as I climb
