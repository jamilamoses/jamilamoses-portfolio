What's Next🔮

Where I am pointed next, as a Solution Engineer, an MBA candidate, and a leader in the community.

My POV💭

The Solution Engineer's role is moving from demonstrating what software can do to proving what an autonomous agent will do inside a customer's business. I intend to lead from the front of that shift.

Discovery still wins

Agentic AI raises the ceiling on how fast and how specifically we can prove value, but the work still starts with the customer's problem. The SEs who win will be the ones who ask the sharper questions.

Reusable over heroic

Scaling as an SE is not about closing more of my own deals. It is about building the frameworks, demos, and enablement the whole team can run.

Trust is the product

In an AI-forward sale the customer is buying confidence as much as capability. The SE's credibility is what closes the distance between a demo and a decision.
